## 6. Separate Sports and Pricing for Each Community

Each community will see only its own sports list and its own prices. For example, one community may offer swimming, basketball and chess, while another offers cricket, chess and skating, and the same sport may have different prices. A resident of one community must never see another community's prices.

> **Example only — not real FITPRO prices.**
> Community A: Swimming ₹2,000/month • Basketball ₹1,500/month • Chess ₹800/month
> Community B: Basketball ₹1,800/month • Cricket ₹1,400/month • Chess ₹700/month • Skating ₹1,200/month
> A Community A resident sees only Community A's list. A Community B resident sees only Community B's list.

### 6.1 Options for Identifying a Resident's Community

#### Option 1 — Invitation link

- **How it works:** FITPRO or the community committee sends each resident a personal link by SMS or email.
- **Good points:** Most secure; one tap for residents; good for launching in a new community.
- **Watch-outs:** Needs the community's resident list; new residents need an invitation.

#### Option 2 — Community code

- **How it works:** Each community gets a code (for example, shared on the notice board) that residents enter when signing up.
- **Good points:** Very simple to share.
- **Watch-outs:** Codes can be passed to outsiders, so they should be combined with approval.

#### Option 3 — Community web link / QR code

- **How it works:** Each community has its own page link or QR code in the clubhouse.
- **Good points:** Easy to find; great for posters.
- **Watch-outs:** Anyone can open a link, so it should never show prices by itself.

#### Option 4 — Approval by community admin

- **How it works:** The resident gives their block and flat number, and the community admin approves.
- **Good points:** Confirms the person really lives there.
- **Watch-outs:** Residents wait for approval; the admin needs to respond quickly.

> **Recommendation:** invite residents with a personal link (Option 1), and let anyone else request access with their flat number for the community admin to approve (Option 4). The community code and QR code can lead residents to the sign-up page, but prices appear only after the resident is approved.

### 6.2 Display Rules

- **Before verification:** Visitors see sport names, descriptions and photos, but no prices, batches or seat counts.
- **After verification:** Residents see their own community's sports, batches, seats left and prices.
- **Sport not offered:** If a sport isn't offered in the resident's community: "This sport isn't available in [Community Name] yet. [Suggest It for My Community]"
- **Price changes:** New prices apply to new bookings and renewals; existing paid packages are not affected. [Policy to be confirmed by FITPRO]

### 6.3 Special Situations

- **More than one community:** Residents who live in or book for more than one community (for example, grandparents) can switch between communities from a "My Community" menu at the top of the page. The sports and prices change to match the community they select.
- **Moving out:** When a resident moves out, the community admin can remove their access. Any active package continues until its end date. [Policy to be confirmed by FITPRO]
- **Tenants and owners:** Both tenants and owners can be verified, as long as the community admin confirms they live there.
- **Guests:** Guests cannot book directly. A verified resident may book for a family member using Family Members.

### 6.4 Screen Text for Community Verification

- **Find your community:** Find Your Community — Search by community name or area.
- **Verify:** Confirm you live here. Enter your community code (if you have one), block/tower and flat number. [Submit]
- **Waiting for approval:** Thank you! Your request has been sent to your community administrator. We'll notify you by SMS once you're approved.
- **Approved:** Welcome to [Community Name]! You can now see the sports and packages available in your community. [View Sports]
- **Not approved:** We couldn't verify your membership. Please contact your community administrator.
- **Wrong code:** That code doesn't look right. Please check with your community office.
- **Invitation expired:** This invitation has expired. Please ask your community administrator to send a new one.
- **Community paused:** Bookings for this community are paused at the moment. Please contact your community administrator.
- **Access removed:** Your access to [Community Name] has been removed. If you think this is a mistake, please contact your community administrator.
